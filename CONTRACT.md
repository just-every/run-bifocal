# Product contract

Target edition: 20 October 2026. **Bifocal** is company-wide business intelligence and reporting for US businesses, replacing Microsoft Power BI, Tableau and Looker while competing with Metabase, Omni, Sigma Computing and Hex. It also serves the separately bought customer reporting portal and embedded analytics job. A first buyer has 20–1,000 employees, 5–500 reporting readers, and often no warehouse; a COO, finance and RevOps lead or small data team needs answers, shared definitions, fresh dashboards, printable reports and accountable follow-up.

Target clauses describe the complete product destination. Published clauses retain two existing done judgments for the current public doors; the full BI product remains unfinished. BIF-045 has native numerical/replay evidence; BIF-003/036/057/058 are partial. The repository contains Base foundation, owner-only file/browser intake, native company conventions and exact saved tabular answers. Provider sandbox, general jobs and the remaining BI workflows are unfinished. Existing desk IDs BIF-001–030 and the Bifocal name and mark are preserved. No provider approval, native distribution, BAA, model-language translation, scale or integration difficulty removes a clause; these are engineering work. Each product remains independent, with sibling integrations and shared code permitted. Contracts and research only: this document changes no homepage, interface or runtime.

## Published clauses

These two clauses retain their existing done judgments and evidence for the current public doors. The remaining product scope stays in Target clauses and Push further.

### Numerical answers

**BIF-045 — Numerical correctness and reproducible answers.** Queries and artifacts record model and metric version, source watermark or snapshot, filters, security scope, calendar and timezone, accounting basis, currency and rate source and rounding. Preserve exact decimal money and distinguish null, zero, unknown, suppressed and incomplete values. Define count-distinct, weighted averages, percentages, non-additive measures, totals after filtering and many-to-many join behavior. Avoid misleading totals from summed ratios, mixed units or inconsistent cutoffs. Cross-source results say when no common snapshot is available and prevent official certification of an unexplained difference. The buyer can rerun the permitted answer for the same immutable snapshot while retained, without disclosing another user's rows.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through query, dashboard and report public doors evaluate a reference dataset with duplicate customer joins, partial refunds, NULLs, negative money, rates and time-bound changes. Compare exact money, distinct counts, weighted margin and subtotal queries to independently written SQL and calculations; rerun an earlier snapshot and reconcile its report after live sources change. Attempt a broader-security replay and require denial.

### Analytics boundaries

**BIF-060 — Analytics market separation.** Bifocal owns the complete cross-system question-to-chart-to-report job and the customer reporting portal. [Vane](https://github.com/just-every/run-vane) owns tracking script, cookie-free collection, traffic dashboards, goals and funnels; Bifocal places no tracking script and reads Vane Stats API and exports. [Porthole](https://github.com/just-every/run-porthole) owns logs, metrics, traces, profiles, SLOs and engineering dashboards; Bifocal does not ingest telemetry and may read business events or usage tables from the product database. Snowflake, BigQuery and Databricks remain general warehouses and lakehouses, Fivetran and Airbyte remain standalone ELT, Segment remains a CDP, and Amplitude and Mixpanel remain product analytics. The Bifocal managed store and sync serves its own reporting and can export to a buyer warehouse; it is not sold as any of those separate products.

**Acceptance evidence:** Walk source setup and a cross-system dashboard at 1440 and 390: read a Vane conversion export and API and a permitted product usage table beside revenue, inspect source links and validate no Bifocal tracker or telemetry ingestion. Export analytical data to a buyer warehouse and show missing warehouse and ELT and CDP and product-analytics setup as a linked separate product, without substituting a reduced reporting product.

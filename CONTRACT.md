# Product contract

Target edition: 20 October 2026. **Bifocal** is company-wide business intelligence and reporting for US businesses, replacing Microsoft Power BI, Tableau and Looker while competing with Metabase, Omni, Sigma Computing and Hex. It also serves the separately bought customer reporting portal and embedded analytics job. A first buyer has 20–1,000 employees, 5–500 reporting readers, and often no warehouse; a COO, finance/RevOps lead or small data team needs answers, shared definitions, fresh dashboards, printable reports and accountable follow-up.

This is the complete product destination, not a published feature claim. All clauses are missing because this new seed has no implementation. Existing desk IDs BIF-001–030 and the Bifocal name/mark are preserved. No provider approval, native distribution, BAA, model-language translation, scale or integration difficulty removes a clause; these are engineering work. Each product remains independent, with sibling integrations and shared code permitted. Contracts and research only: this document changes no homepage, interface or runtime.

## Acceptance conventions

Every clause states a public-door journey, not an internal helper assertion. Browser journeys use the real built product at **1440 and 390 CSS pixels** with realistic USD finance, sales, support, work and payroll data, permissions and failure/empty/loading states; inspect resulting persisted data and rendered/exported artifacts. Native apps and add-ins use their supported real host applications and devices. No screens or product code exist to exercise in this contract-only drafting task, so these are future acceptance requirements, not tests claimed run today.

Provider evidence uses an authorized agency/provider sandbox, or a fake only at the paid/network edge built from a cited official provider protocol/schema. No mocked application, invented provider success or fixture establishes live acceptance. Record source/schema version, granted fields, environment, expected and observed results, artifacts and limitations. Real migration estates are buyer-authorized. Measured performance includes workload, compute, network, dataset shape and exact freshness/cache state. Every capability inherits scoped access, audit, portability, retention/erasure, US custody, costs and human approvals. An unknown outcome remains unknown. Performance/accuracy figures below are acceptance targets, not present claims.

## Target clauses

### Owner operation and US defaults

**BIF-031 — Owner-run installation through Base.** A buyer installs and upgrades Bifocal through Base into their own Cloudflare account, on an owner-selected domain, with their own data, storage, secrets and authorized analytical compute. The supported install, setup, health, upgrade, export, restore and removal paths are documented and usable without the author or marketplace remaining online. Cloudflare hosts the application/control plane; large columnar queries, Python and private-network work use declared, owner-authorized analytical jobs or outbound runners with bounded resources. This architecture must deliver BIF-004/023, not replace their scale with D1-sized reporting. Setup identifies US location, account, dependencies, scopes, expected USD costs and unfinished provider approvals. Native work remains usable when optional connectors fail. No telemetry, licence callback, author kill switch or undisclosed external data flow is required. Public Lobby/docs, private app, setup and the separate run site have distinct routes and custody.

**Acceptance evidence:** From an empty buyer-owned Cloudflare account run the supported Base install and setup, sign in at the app origin and open its public docs signed out. Connect a real source, populate content, upgrade through schema migrations, inspect health with a failed analytical dependency, export, remove and restore on a second owner installation. Block author/marketplace egress and prove native reporting remains usable. Inspect private-route refusal and declared external traffic.

**BIF-032 — Sign-in and active membership.** Base native sign-in and revocable owner sessions work from first setup. Optional Auth supplies SAML/OIDC, MFA and SCIM, with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace provider configurations. Only invited active members or explicitly provisioned portal users receive access. Verified identity maps to installation membership, roles and analytics attributes; an email domain alone is no grant. Admins manage invitations, groups, sessions, ownership transfer and emergency recovery without bypassing row security. Revoke membership or a session and subsequent reads, exports, scheduled jobs and delegated calls fail closed. Browser mutations enforce session, origin and CSRF protections; machine credentials use BIF-035. The last owner cannot strand an installation by deleting their only recovery path.

**Acceptance evidence:** Use actual setup/sign-in routes to invite admin, modeller, creator and viewer; sign in natively and through Auth with each named identity provider using its sandbox or official OIDC/SAML/SCIM schema edge. Reject an uninvited identity, forged assertion, CSRF request and stale session; remove a group/member while a query/export is queued and prove its next authorization fails. Transfer ownership and recover access through the documented owner path.

**BIF-033 — Complete portable archives and inactive restore.** Authenticated UI and Base CLI export every product record: source definitions and sync watermarks/history, imported files and versions, managed data or a complete integrity-checked data manifest, transforms, semantic definitions and certification history, git/environment mappings, queries/notebooks, charts, dashboards/reports, schedules, delivery outcomes, annotations, goals, input-table edits, portal configuration, grants as inactive definitions, usage, agent proposals, approvals and anonymized audit. Large exports resume and report completeness; never truncate. Validate schema, references and content digests before restoring into eligible empty product state. Installation-local sessions, OAuth/source secrets, signing keys and agent/portal capabilities do not travel. A new owner reconnects sources and reauthorizes access. Restored schedules/actions stay paused and delivered history never replays. Portable erasure fences prevent later restores from reviving previously erased subjects when the current erasure journal is supplied.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through both authenticated archive doors export an installation containing every target record class and file bytes; restore into a separately initialized owner account, re-export and compare domain counts, definitions, history and file digests. Interrupt/resume a large export; reject a corrupt, incomplete or nonempty restore without partial writes. Confirm secrets absent, all dispatch paused and a later erasure journal blocks resurrection.

**BIF-034 — Retention and erasure across analytical copies.** Product history persists until an owner explicitly archives or erases it or enables a disclosed retention rule. Archiving is reversible and never silently improves freshness, adoption or correctness statistics. Owner-directed subject/source/tenant erasure covers managed tables, uploaded originals/versions, caches, aggregates, search, extracts, notebook outputs, generated reports, prompts, drafts, mobile snapshots under application control and queued deliveries. Track completion per storage/compute/provider, disclose legal holds and provider-retained copies, and return a receipt only for completed work. Retain a permanent minimal audit with anonymized actor/subject references, not erased rows or PII. Mark deleted identities in the sync fence so stale events/imports do not recreate them; future authorized source ingestion follows a disclosed deletion policy. External downloads and sent messages cannot be recalled and are identified as such.

**Acceptance evidence:** Erase a customer appearing in joins, an uploaded sheet, cached answer, report attachment, notebook, portal and queued review through the owner route. Search/query/export and invoke agents after completion, replay an old sync page, and restore an older archive with the current journal: no erased content returns. Inspect anonymized audit, per-store receipt, provider-pending state and legal-hold explanation. Restore an archived dashboard separately.

**BIF-035 — Agents and services as scoped credentials.** Agents, connectors, runners and automation clients are named credentials with an accountable human or service owner, purpose, installation/tenant, allowed sources/models/content/tools, expiry, budget and revocation. Effective access is the intersection of the credential scope, current principal access and source permissions, checked server-side on every operation and queued execution. No prompt, model output, imported document or tool result grants authority. SQL/Python execution does not obtain connection secrets or general network access. Credential rotation and denial are visible and audited. Approval is a separate human action bound to exact artifact, recipients, policy and data/model versions; agents cannot mint, inherit from a restore, or approve their own grants.

**Acceptance evidence:** Issue a West-only read credential via the admin API, query through REST/GraphQL/MCP and try East, unapproved tools, expired/revoked authority and a malicious instruction in a source cell. Suspend its human owner during queued work. Inspect refusal without side effects, redacted diagnostics, attribution and distinct human approval events; restore an archive and prove the credential is inactive.

**BIF-036 — US dates, money, calendars and accessible conventions.** First setup defaults to USD, MM/DD/YYYY and America/New_York organization schedules; viewers choose their own timezone. Keep source instants and currency, display zone/offset and report cutoff, and define DST skip/repeat behavior before saving schedules. Support any fiscal-year start, 4-4-5 and 4-5-4 retail calendars including 53-week years, US federal holidays and working-day measures. Multi-currency reports retain original amounts and use a named licensed/provider or owner-imported rate source, rate date, conversion policy and rounding; mixed currencies never sum without conversion. US maps include states, counties, ZIP codes, DMAs, congressional districts and points with licensed/versioned boundaries. WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 accessibility apply to all workflows, including tables, exports and phone readers; a chart always has an accessible data equivalent.

**Acceptance evidence:** At 1440 and 390 create a US company, a July-start and a retail fiscal calendar, a holiday-adjusted measure and a USD report including EUR transactions. Compare leap day, 53-week retail year, rate/date rounding and DST transition executions to fixed independent calculations. Navigate setup, chart, table, report and errors by keyboard/screen reader, inspect contrast/reflow and open each US map geography.

**BIF-037 — Predictable reporting cost and owner budgets.** Show USD installation, storage, compute, refresh, delivery, external-model and per-portal-tenant usage with measured quantities and clearly labeled estimates. Pricing does not meter AI per prompt or token; infrastructure and third-party charges are disclosed separately without disguising them as unlimited free work. Owners set source, job, agent and tenant budgets, receive warnings, inspect cost attribution and pause expensive work before approval. A held estimate names its uncertainty and recovery path; enforcement uses real execution reservations and post-run reconciliation, not an estimate treated as a guaranteed bill. Portal-reader costs and capacity limits are understandable before adding customers.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through Settings configure source, agent and tenant USD budgets, run representative refreshes/queries/reports, hit each budget, and reconcile usage to provider sandbox invoices or official usage schema edges. Check one expensive query is held before execution and becomes runnable after an authorized budget change; repeated natural-language questions create no per-prompt line item.

Bifocal signs people in without passwords: by a link that `rer dev` prints on your computer, or by a link sent to
their email address.

## On your own computer

When `rer dev` starts Bifocal it prints `RER_OPEN` and a private link. Open that link in a browser on the same
computer and Bifocal opens signed in as the owner. Treat it like a password: anyone who has it can sign in as you.

To switch to another person while you try Bifocal out, invite them first (see
[People and your settings](/docs/people)), then choose **Sign in as** beside their name on the People page or on the
sign-in page. On your own computer and in demos, Bifocal sends no email at all.

## By email link

On an installation that sends mail, the sign-in page asks for your email address. Choose **Email a sign-in link**,
then open the link the message carries. Only people who were invited can sign in; any other address gets no
access.

## The front page and the app

The Lobby's **Sign in** button opens the sign-in page. Once signed in, the app home is at `/app` on your
installation, with links to your settings and, for owners and admins, to People.

## Sign out

At the bottom of every app page, choose **Sign out**. Bifocal ends your session and returns you to the sign-in page.

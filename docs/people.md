Owners and admins add the people who use Bifocal. Every person signs in as themselves and has one role.

## Roles

- **Owner**: the person who set Bifocal up. Owners invite members and admins, and open identity settings.
- **Admin**: invites members and sees everyone's role and status.
- **Member**: signs in and uses the app.

These are the foundation's roles. Analytics roles, groups and row-level security are part of the
[contract](/contract) and are not built yet.

## Invite a person

Open **People** from the app home. Under **Invite a person**, enter their name and email address, choose a role and
choose **Send invitation**. Admins can invite members; owners can also invite admins.

The People page lists everyone with their email address, role and status. If the person already has an account or a
different invitation, Bifocal says so instead of sending a second one.

On your own computer and in demos Bifocal sends no email: choose **Sign in as** beside the person to accept their
invitation and open the app as them.

## Your settings

Open **Your settings** from the app home to change the name your account shows, from 1 to 120 characters. Your email
address and your access stay the same.
